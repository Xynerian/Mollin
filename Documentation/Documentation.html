Hello.

This is the documentation for a project I'm currently working on, called Mollins. In this simulation/game you watch over generations of Mollins as they evolve and adapt to their environment (hopefully, atleast).

The inputs.

When you open the simulation/game you are greeted with some parameters like the Maximum Health, Maximum Temp, Minimum Temp, etc. This section will tell you what each of these does.

Maximum Health

The maximum health of a Mollin.

Maximum Temperature

The highest temperature a Mollin can survive in before it starts taking damage.

Minimum Temperature

The lowest temperature a Mollin can survive in before it starts taking damage.

Lifetime

The lifespan of a Mollin in hours(In Mollin time. Learn more about that here).

Maximum Food

The maximum amount of food a Mollin can store before it starts taking damage due to over-eating.

Note: This might be a bit tricky to remember, but the hunger isn't actually how hungry the Mollin is, it's how much food it has.

Food Chance

The chance that a mollin will find food every hour. This is a percentage.

Note: All of the above values will gradually change as the mollin mutates.

Status Value's

This will hopefully give you a good enough understanding of what the different status values mean.

Health

The health of a Mollin, when this reaches zero, the Mollin dies and either the species goes extinct or, if the dead mollin lived through atleast a quarter of it's lifetime, the death event triggers.

Hunger

The amount of food a Mollin currently has.

Temperature

The temperature of a Mollin. If this goes below the minimum temperature it will take damage equal to the difference between the current temperature and the minimum temperature. The same applies to going over the maximum.

Age

Each unit represents one hour of a Mollin's life. To you, each unit is the tick speed. The tick speed can be viewed and changed in settings (this symbol ) at the top of the web page.

Events

Events happen when certain conditions are met. They are usually not part of every tick.

Finding Food

Every tick there is a chance (the Food Chance) that the mollin will find food equal to it's Food Chance divided by 10 plus 1.

Reproduction

When a mollin's age reaches half of it's lifetime, and it's hunger stat is atleast half of it's Maximum Food attribute and it's age is less than 3/4 of it's lifetime, it will reproduce.

Death

When a mollin's health reaches zero, it dies, but, if it lived through at least a quarter of it's lifetime, it's parent generation continues.
